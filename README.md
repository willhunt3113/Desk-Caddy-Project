## Project_goal

The goal of this project was to create a functional desk caddy in Autodesk Inventor. 

Some of the requirements for this caddy were: 

-  Hold pens and/or pencils
-  Hold scissors or another tall desk tool
-  Include a compartment or way to store sticky notes
-  Include at least one additional storage area for small objects

## Planning


The first step of this project was to find Caddys that you liked the design of.  

This was the first design that stood out to me from [Wynnfell](https://www.wynnfell.com/products/basupply-1pc-desktop-pen-holder-stationery-holder-pencil-cosmetics-organizer-for-desk-office-school-storage-case-accessories). 

<img width="400" height="400" alt="Wynfell Caddy" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/756994db-965d-41fa-a46e-ad715ee08d25" />

I liked the sliding drawer and the compartment for your phone. I plan to add something similar to mine. Something that I did not like was the height of the phone compartment. I want the items in the compartment to be more visible and easy to reach. For my design the the compartments of my caddy will be more shallow to give the user easier access. This design was 225x10x12 mm which will be similar to mine. This design is featured to hold a phone, writing utensils, a stapler, and any small tools that could fit in the compartment. From this design I plan to take the sliding drawer and the phone holder.

### What features make a desk organizer useful and easy to use?

To me accesability is the most important function for a desk caddy. Making sure that the user has access to all of the tools that they need in a quick mannor is the main function to me. Look is also important for a desk caddy. This is something that the user will looking at throughout the whole day so it must be something that looks good as well. A design that I notice for most desk caddys is elevated levels so that everything in visible and in reach. This is something that I will incorporate into my design. A usefull desk caddy has compartments for all of the tools that the user needs. They should be in sight and easily reachable.
