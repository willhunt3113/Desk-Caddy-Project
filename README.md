## Planning

The first step of this project was to find Caddys that you liked the design of.  

This was the first design that stood out to me from ![Wynnfell](https://www.wynnfell.com/products/basupply-1pc-desktop-pen-holder-stationery-holder-pencil-cosmetics-organizer-for-desk-office-school-storage-case-accessories). 

<img width="902" height="902" alt="Wynfell Caddy" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/756994db-965d-41fa-a46e-ad715ee08d25" />

I liked the sliding drawer and the compartment for your phone. I plan to add something similar to mine. Something that I did not like was the height of the phone compartment. I want the items in the compartment to be more visible and easy to reach. For my design the walls of my caddy will be at a lower height.
